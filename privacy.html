gmail-curator privacy policy

gmail-curator is a private, personal-use script. One person runs it on their own computer to organise their own Gmail mailbox and to save their own documents to their own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What this policy covers

  • What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail messages, labels and filters, and only those Google Drive files the script itself created.
  • Where data is kept: on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with other people.
  • AI assistance: the owner uses an AI assistant (Claude, from Anthropic) to help run the script, and the assistant sees run reports and message details such as subjects and senders in the course of that work.
  • Deletion: the script cannot permanently delete mail and never requests that permission.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove the script's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
